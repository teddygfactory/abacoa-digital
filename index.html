A few questions
before we start.

Useful things to know when you’re deciding what belongs in your business.

Can this work with the tools we already use?

That is part of the discovery conversation. We review your CRM, calendar, forms and team tools, then confirm which connections are practical before agreeing the scope. Bring the names of the tools you want to keep.

Will an AI assistant make decisions for me?

You define its responsibilities. A system can answer questions, collect information and act within agreed rules, with owner review or a human handoff for the situations you choose. We work through those boundaries during the build.

Are the businesses in the demonstrations clients?

The homepage stories and dashboard are illustrative examples with sample data. Briar, Bramble and Polish are our own products. They show the kind of work and thinking we can bring to your project.

Can I speak with the person building my system?

Yes. Mike leads the project conversation, scope and build at Abacoa Digital. Our AI receptionist can capture your inquiry when you call. You can also email Mike directly.

What if I already have a project with you?

Use the existing-client route below. Include your business, the system involved, what changed and the impact. Prepare a project update or support request.
