Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2, 2026

Abacoa Digital LLC ("Abacoa Digital," "we," "us") builds AI and software systems for small businesses. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices. It applies to abacoadigital.com and to our phone, email, and text (SMS) communications.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us — name, business name, email, phone number, and anything you type into our contact form or tell us on a call.
  • Communication records — the content of messages, calls, and emails between you and us, including AI-assisted voice calls, which may be recorded and transcribed so we can follow up accurately.
  • Basic site data — standard analytics such as pages viewed and device type, used only to improve the site.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry, qualify your project, and follow up.
  • To send you service-related messages by email or SMS (for example, confirming we received your request or coordinating a call).
  • To provide, operate, and improve the services you engage us for.
SMS / text messaging. If you provide your phone number or reply to one of our texts, you may receive service and follow-up messages from Abacoa Digital. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. Consenting to receive texts is never a condition of purchasing anything from us.

We do not sell or share your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in consent with any third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes. Text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with anyone.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate (for example, our hosting, email, telephony, and AI infrastructure vendors), and only as needed to deliver the service. We may disclose information if required by law. Mobile opt-in and SMS consent data are excluded from all sharing, as stated above.

Data retention & security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and we use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

Your choices

  • Opt out of texts anytime by replying STOP.
  • Unsubscribe from emails using the link in any message, or by contacting us.
  • Request that we access, correct, or delete your information by emailing us.
